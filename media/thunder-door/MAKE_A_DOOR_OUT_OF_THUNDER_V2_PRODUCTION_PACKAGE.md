# Make a Door Out of Thunder — Definitive Story Cut v2

## Voice-readable summary

This is the production-locked animatic package for the Thunder-Door film lane. It is no longer exploratory. The purpose is to create a 90–120 second handmade stop-motion cinematic story cut using the newest dark room / little girl / gold thread / ghost / portal visual canon.

## Guiding sentence

A little girl learns to stitch lightning into a door, not to escape fear, but to transform the room that held it.

## Core locks

- Canon images: newest dark illustrated room / little girl / gold thread / ghost / portal visuals.
- Animation level: Level 2.5 — handmade stop-motion illusion plus cinematic compositing.
- Runtime: 90–120 seconds.
- Vocals: not yet.
- Audio: cinematic instrumental score plus lyric-fragment captions.
- Ending: B/C hybrid — she transforms the room and leaves the glowing thread behind.
- ARC/toolchain logic: hidden as magic behavior, not visible interface/system language.
- Visual priority: authored, tactile, emotionally readable, not generic AI fantasy.

Everything derives from this visual family:

## Story beat sheet

### Act I — The Room Listens

1. The title appears over a dark room breathing with rainlight.
2. The room is too quiet, almost listening.
3. Rain taps the window like small code the girl cannot yet read.
4. The girl sits still, small against the room.
5. A gold thread appears where the floor should be ordinary.
6. She reaches toward it but does not pull yet.
7. A ghost appears in the mirror or room edge, witnessing rather than commanding.
8. The girl turns toward the ghost and understands that the room is waiting for her action.

### Act II — The Room Becomes a Map

9. The floor begins glowing under the thread.
10. The glow spreads into a map made of memory, rain, and broken lines.
11. The ghost points toward the wall where no door exists.
12. The girl kneels with the thread.
13. She traces a structure across the floor.
14. The thread pulls toward the storm-facing wall.
15. She tries to form a door.
16. The first door fails, collapsing into sparks and loose thread.

### Act III — Stitching the Door

17. The girl regathers the thread.
18. Her fear turns into resolve.
19. Lightning gathers outside the window.
20. She stitches lightning into the frame.
21. The storm door stabilizes, no longer a hole but a made thing.
22. The door opens into a golden portal.

### Act IV — Transformation

23. The girl crosses the threshold.
24. The room remains behind, changed, with a glowing thread still alive on the floor.

## Canon notes

### Girl

- Small but not helpless.
- Silhouette must remain consistent across shots.
- Emotional sequence: stillness → hesitation → fear → failure → resolve → agency → quiet power.
- Movement should be limited but meaningful: reach, kneel, gather, stitch, step.

### Ghost

- Witness / guide, not savior.
- Should not dominate the frame.
- Best appearances: mirror, room edge, soft reflection, pointing gesture.
- Its function is to indicate direction, not solve the problem.

### Gold thread

- Living signal.
- Moves like a mix of thread, lightning, and memory.
- It should never look like a generic laser beam.
- It teaches through tension, pull, knots, recoils, and glow.

### Door

- Made, not found.
- The first version fails.
- The final version stabilizes only after the girl changes her relationship to the room.
- The door should feel handmade out of storm material and gold thread.

### Room

- A holding structure, not merely a prison.
- It begins as fear-space and becomes transformed witness-space.
- The ending room should not be empty in a dead way. It should be quieter because it has changed.

## Animation instructions per shot

General method:

- Use still image plates as base.
- Animate with camera drift, parallax layers, texture flicker, rain overlays, particle passes, thread masks, glow animation, and limited pose swaps.
- Character movement should be sparse but exact.
- Favor tactile stop-motion imperfection over smooth synthetic animation.

Shot-specific motion priorities:

- Shots 01–04: slow breathing room, rain motion, minimal girl motion.
- Shots 05–08: thread pulse, first hand reach, ghost reveal, head turn.
- Shots 09–14: floor-map line drawing, ghost gesture, kneeling pose, thread tension.
- Shots 15–16: unstable door, collapse, sparks, silence break.
- Shots 17–18: thread regather, face/hand resolve beat.
- Shots 19–22: lightning, stitching, door stabilization, portal bloom.
- Shots 23–24: step cycle, threshold light, final transformed room pullback.

## Sound design and instrumental score plan

### Overall sound world

- Piano or celeste motif for gold thread.
- Low bowed strings or synth drone for room pressure.
- Soft rain throughout, changing intensity by act.
- Distant thunder as structural punctuation.
- Thread sounds: fine metallic whisper, not sci-fi laser.
- Door sounds: wood, storm pressure, breath, metal tension, low harmonic bloom.

### Score arc

1. Opening: sparse piano/celeste over rain.
2. Thread appearance: introduce high gold motif.
3. Floor-map: add pulsing low strings and quiet percussion.
4. Failure: sudden harmonic fracture, brief drop to near silence.
5. Regather: motif returns slower and stronger.
6. Stitching: thunder percussion plus rising strings.
7. Portal: full warm harmonic opening.
8. Ending: reduce to thread motif, rain softened.
