# Independent Creative Web

This surface exists to make self-contained web artworks, instruments, toys, and small worlds.

## Identity boundary

It is deliberately not:

- a Mirror Cartographer explanation page;
- a research portal;
- an operations dashboard;
- a product funnel;
- a therapeutic, diagnostic, or scientific claim surface.

## Creative rules

1. Each work must have a complete premise understandable without project context.
2. Interaction must produce an immediate perceptible consequence.
3. Sound is optional, user-initiated, and never required for comprehension.
4. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
5. Works should remain readable and operable on touch, keyboard, and narrow screens.
6. Each slice must be independently reversible by commit or path removal without modifying Mirror Cartographer runtime files.

## Current work

**Matchbox Weather** is a pocket-sized generative weather instrument. A visitor strikes the surface to produce ash, rain, moths, or electrical static. It is a small sensory object, not an explanation of another system.
