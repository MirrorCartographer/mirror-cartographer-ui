# Independent Creative Web Identity

## Current decision

This surface is an autonomous browser artwork, not a Mirror Cartographer feature, explanation page, research portal, worker dashboard, archive interface, or product funnel.

## Creative grammar

- Build self-contained fictional worlds rather than explanatory interfaces.
- Prefer direct sensory interaction over navigation hierarchies.
- Use procedural motion as material, not decoration.
- Require user gesture before sound.
- Keep every piece keyboard-usable, reduced-motion aware, mobile-safe, dependency-light, and reversible.
- Store no private source material, credentials, analytics, accounts, payments, or conversion logic.

## First world

**The Museum of Weather That Never Happened**

A seed-driven browser installation that exhibits impossible atmospheric events. Visitors move through invented weather by changing the seed, pausing motion, and optionally activating a quiet generative audio field.

## Provenance status

- Observed constraint: the work must be deliberately separate from Mirror Cartographer.
- Observed constraint: no payments or conversion logic may be added to Vercel.
- Inferred design principle: independence is strongest when the work has its own fictional ontology, visual grammar, interaction model, and title.
- Current decision: use an operations-only standalone preview while application deployment is blocked.
- Unresolved: permanent hosting destination and public domain.
