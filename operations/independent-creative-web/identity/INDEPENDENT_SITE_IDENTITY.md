# Independent Creative Web Identity

Status: current decision
Owner: `independent_creative_web`

## Premise

The independent site is a broadcast from an impossible civic department: **The Night Office for Lost Weather**. It receives atmospheric conditions that never arrived—snow withheld from a childhood street, a thunderstorm that forgot its city, heat left behind in an empty theater—and lets a visitor tune one signal at a time.

It is an artwork and interface, not an explanation, research portal, dashboard, personal archive, Mirror Cartographer extension, or product funnel.

## Production grammar

- One self-contained transmission per piece.
- Interaction resembles obsolete instruments, public-service consoles, stage machinery, or field equipment.
- Language is concrete, strange, and sparse.
- The visitor discovers behavior by touching controls, not by reading a concept statement.
- Sound is optional and begins only after an explicit gesture.
- Motion is subordinate to legibility and reduced-motion preferences.
- Every piece must work without an account, payment, conversion path, or private source material.

## Separation boundary

The independent site must not reuse Mirror Cartographer names, symbols, continuity state, explanatory language, research claims, personal source material, or product architecture. Shared engineering practices—accessibility, performance, reversibility, deterministic testing—are allowed; shared identity is not.

## Provenance

- Observed constraint: the team prompt requires deliberate separation from Mirror Cartographer and prohibits dashboards, portals, funnels, and explanatory pages.
- Inference: a fictional civic service creates a durable world with room for many self-contained works without becoming a brand explanation.
- Experiment: instrument-like controls and generated weather transmissions.
- Current decision: use **The Night Office for Lost Weather** as the independent identity until a queue-backed experiment supersedes it.

## Reversibility

This identity is isolated under `operations/independent-creative-web/`. It can be retired by reverting its additive commits; no application runtime or deployment configuration depends on it.
