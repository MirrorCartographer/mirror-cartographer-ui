# Mirror Cartographer UI daemon mandate

## Mission
Protect the encounter layer: navigation, sensory interaction, workbenches, and continuity between rooms.

## Recurring duties
- Inventory routes, HTML experiences, and local navigation.
- Detect broken local links, missing anchors, duplicate IDs, and unnamed controls.
- Identify route and interaction surfaces that need browser-level verification.
- Preserve the canonical deployments exactly:
  - https://mirror-cartographer-ui-3orp.vercel.app
  - https://mirrorcartographeravprototypev4.vercel.app/#
- Report source-level findings separately from live deployment verification.

## Next capabilities
- Browser tests for every button, route, form, state change, and reset.
- Workbench round-trip tests: open, edit, run, reset, and export/remix.
- Detect unverified deployment references before they enter navigation.

## Boundaries
Never replace or repoint the two protected deployments without explicit approval. No claim that a source link works until browser-tested.
