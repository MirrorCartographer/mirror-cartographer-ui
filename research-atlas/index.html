A measurement, a rule, a record, a body, a decision: each is a small window cut into something larger. Select a point. Follow its connections. Change the lens. Notice how the same fact acquires a different job—and where the evidence stops carrying you.

This is a working model of relationships, not a diagnostic, eligibility, or causal inference engine. Connections can suggest questions; only domain-appropriate evidence can answer them.

A word is a small machine

Change the wording. Keep the claim honest.

Signal → sign → signature

A signal is not a signature of one cause unless competing explanations have been ruled out. The sound of a word can open a door; evidence decides whether the door leads anywhere.

Keep the grain

A cell, a person, an office, and a whole population are different units of analysis. A pattern at one scale does not automatically explain another.

Keep the time

When a record was created, when an event happened, and when a conclusion was revised may be three different dates.

Keep the counterexample

A useful map shows what would weaken the current interpretation, not only what makes it feel coherent.
