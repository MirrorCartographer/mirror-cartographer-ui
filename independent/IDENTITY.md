# Independent Creative Web — Identity Boundary

This surface exists to make self-contained digital pieces, instruments, rooms, performances, and small worlds.

It is not a Mirror Cartographer explanation page, research portal, worker dashboard, continuity interface, or product funnel. It must not reuse private source material, diagnostic language, project-management grammar, payment logic, or conversion mechanics.

## Current decision

Prefer experiences that are complete in one encounter: locally generated, non-extractive, reversible, accessible, mobile-safe, and understandable without account creation.

## Provenance classes

- **Observed:** prior requests favored dynamic, hand-made, spatial, surprising interfaces and working interactions.
- **Inference:** a separate studio benefits from atmosphere, play, and ambiguity rather than explanation.
- **Experiment:** `weatherless-window` tests whether a single-file generative instrument can feel alive without persistence, external services, audio autoplay, or project vocabulary.
- **Experiment:** `fourth-room` tests whether a narrative room can deepen through repeated local interaction, session-scoped memory, and optional user-initiated sound without becoming a product, explanation surface, or identity system.
- **Experiment:** `unfinished-constellation` tests whether a deterministic spatial instrument can produce changing interpretations through direct manipulation without storage, sound, networking, or explanatory system language.
- **Experiment:** `house-that-refuses-noon` tests whether fixed architecture can feel mutable when only illumination and route legibility change, using pointer and keyboard manipulation without storage, networking, sound, or explanatory system language.
- **Current decision:** keep the independent surface structurally and linguistically separate from Mirror Cartographer; each piece lives in its own additive directory and must remain independently reversible.

## Creative grammar

- Build places, instruments, games, films, rituals, or impossible objects rather than dashboards.
- Let interaction reveal the premise; explanatory copy is secondary.
- Prefer authored constraint over infinite generative sameness.
- Sound is optional, user-initiated, and never required for comprehension.
- Motion has a reduced-motion equivalent.
- A work may remember only what it needs for the immediate visit unless a future queue item explicitly justifies durable storage.
- No commerce, lead capture, authentication, analytics, or behavioral profiling.
