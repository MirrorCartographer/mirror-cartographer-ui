# Independent Creative Web — Identity Boundary

This surface exists to make self-contained digital pieces, instruments, rooms, performances, and small worlds.

It is not a Mirror Cartographer explanation page, research portal, worker dashboard, continuity interface, or product funnel. It must not reuse private source material, diagnostic language, project-management grammar, payment logic, or conversion mechanics.

## Current decision

Prefer experiences that are complete in one encounter: locally generated, non-extractive, reversible, accessible, mobile-safe, and understandable without account creation.

## Provenance classes

- **Observed:** prior requests favored dynamic, hand-made, spatial, surprising interfaces and working interactions.
- **Inference:** a separate studio benefits from atmosphere, play, and ambiguity rather than explanation.
- **Experiment:** `weatherless-window` tests whether a single-file generative instrument can feel alive without persistence, external services, audio autoplay, or project vocabulary.
- **Current decision:** keep the independent surface structurally and linguistically separate from Mirror Cartographer.
