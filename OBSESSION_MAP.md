# The Obsession Map
*A trail through rooms, words, weather, and the strange life between things*

This repository is not a product catalog. It is a set of encounters: some playable, some linguistic, some sonic, some still in the workshop. Each one approaches a different part of the same human problem—how to experience a pattern before we know what to call it, and how to keep the name from replacing the experience.

Start with the thing that catches your eye. Follow it until it changes meaning.

## Door 01 — The Room-World
**A room that responds before it explains.**

The experiential interface is the closest thing here to entering a thought rather than reading about one. The world is built around atmosphere, interaction, and changing states—not a conventional dashboard.

Begin at [the application source](src/main.jsx) and [the Room-World component](src/components/RoomWorldValid.jsx). Follow the room catalog and audio logic from there. The intended design language includes rain, murmur, aurora, dawn, wind, lightning, cloud states, visual memory, story threads, animated heart geometry, phrase contours, and rhythm.

Those are experiential materials, not mere background decoration: weather can express a state; a phrase can leave a contour; a moment can become a mark that helps continuity survive.

## Door 02 — The Living Atlas
**A map that should feel like a world, not a sitemap.**

The atlas is the larger field of work: rooms, games, research, creative pieces, tools, and the relationships among them.

Open [the atlas source](public/atlas/index.html). Read its paths as invitations. When a link points to a missing, stale, or nonfunctional destination, that is a repair item—not an invitation to pretend the door opened.

## Door 03 — The Between
**Change the path. Change what becomes visible.**

[Open the source](research-atlas/index.html).

The Between is an instrument for exploring relationships through different lenses and traversal paths. Its central tension is productive: connection is exciting, but connection alone does not prove cause. A useful instrument lets you reverse the path, inspect the link, change a condition, and see what no longer holds.

## Door 04 — Matchbox Weather
**A small weather system you can hold in language.**

[Enter Matchbox Weather](independent-creative-web/matchbox-weather/index.html).

This is an independent creative piece. Give it room to remain itself. Its weather-score experiments suggest a way to treat atmosphere as composition: not a mood label, but a sequence, pressure, texture, and change.

Explore the neighboring weather-score files and their tests. The tests are part of the artwork's promise: the experience should have a traceable contract, not just a compelling description.

## Door 05 — Make a Door Out of Thunder
**Turn a force into an entrance.**

[Open the production package](media/thunder-door/MAKE_A_DOOR_OUT_OF_THUNDER_V2_PRODUCTION_PACKAGE.md).

Thunder is both sound and event: pressure made audible, distance made temporal, a warning that arrives after its cause. A door is a boundary that can become a passage. Putting them together creates a question: what if the thing that overwhelms you could also mark the threshold to somewhere else?

Follow the production package as a creative artifact. Do not assume it is already a finished film or playable work; distinguish the design from what has actually been produced.

## Door 07 — The language laboratory

The work has repeatedly returned to a few transformations:

- **C → Ca → Cas:** a fragment gathers sound, then begins to become a name.
- **CAS → SAC:** the same pieces reverse; order changes recognition.
- **Ch:** two letters make a new entrance, a hinge of breath.
- **The word as room:** meaning is not only a definition; it is somewhere a mind can move.
- **The contradiction as compass:** opposing descriptions may point toward a missing condition.
- **The phrase as contour:** language has shape, rhythm, pressure, and aftereffects—not only semantic content.

These are creative readings, not claims about historical etymology. Their value is what they let you notice and make.

## Door 08 — The vocabulary that has not finished becoming itself

**Nullglow:** the outline left by an absence that matters.  
**Glyphfracture:** a symbol splitting into readings that cannot be reduced to one.  
**Driftroot:** a recurring pattern whose origin is not yet known.  
**Tonefold:** a change in meaning caused by a shift in tone or phrasing.  
**Lurecraft:** an invitation that creates curiosity without deciding the answer.  
**Mirrorgrain:** the direction in which a reflection distorts what it reflects.  
**Aftermeaning:** the meaning that arrives only after an experience has passed.

These terms are provisional tools. Keep the ones that unlock perception; revise the ones that become precious but unhelpful.

## Door 09 — The memory that keeps a change alive

A living continuity system should preserve more than the final answer. It should keep the path: what happened, what was noticed, what interpretation appeared, what challenged it, what changed, and what remains unresolved.

Explore [mind](mind/), especially its memory, journal, hypotheses, decisions, failures, and graph areas. These folders describe different aspects of the continuity ambition; their presence alone does not prove a complete persistent system is implemented.

## A way to wander

Pick one object. Ask what it changes when you approach from another angle. Follow one relationship. Notice one absence. Make one small artifact: a sentence, a sound, a diagram, a test, a room, a counterexample.

Then leave a trace of what changed in your understanding.

No forced confession. No requirement to turn every feeling into a diagnosis. No requirement to make every metaphor scientifically literal.

**Let the experience open the question. Let evidence decide what can be claimed.**
