# Animal Recovery v6.1 Real Vet Packet Templates

## Voice-readable summary

These templates convert v6.1 into real veterinary communication packets. They are not diagnosis or treatment instructions. They help the user communicate observations, questions, and response data clearly.

## Use order

1. Complete fake QA first if possible.
2. Create one real packet at a time.
3. Start with the highest-priority animal.
4. Keep each packet short enough to read on a phone call.
5. Add objective observations before interpretations.

## O'Malley packet template

### One-sentence concern

O'Malley has enlarged lymph nodes, and I want to identify whether this is cancer-related, viral/immune-related, infectious, inflammatory, or another treatable process.

### Top question

Which next test changes the decision most: lymph-node FNA, FeLV/FIV confirmation, CBC/chemistry/urinalysis, biopsy if FNA is inconclusive, or something else?

### Specific questions

- Are the lymph nodes large enough and accessible enough for FNA?
- If FNA is inconclusive, when would biopsy be recommended?
- Should FeLV/FIV status be repeated or confirmed?
- Should CBC, chemistry, and urinalysis be done before treatment decisions?
- What findings would make this urgent versus scheduled follow-up?

### Response metrics to track

- lymph node size
- firmness
- appetite
- weight
- energy
- hiding
- fever signs
- vomiting/diarrhea
- breathing changes

## Bug / Bugsy packet template

### One-sentence concern

Bug has a heart murmur and glaucoma/eye-pressure concern, and I want to protect vision while checking for systemic contributors such as blood pressure, kidney, thyroid/metabolic, or heart disease.

### Top question

Can we prioritize blood pressure, eye pressure/tonometry, and basic bloodwork, then decide whether heart imaging or specialist referral is needed?

### Specific questions

- What is the current intraocular pressure?
- What is Bug's blood pressure?
- Are kidney values and urinalysis current?
- Is thyroid/metabolic testing indicated based on age/signs?
- What murmur grade was heard?
- What would trigger echo, chest imaging, or referral?
- What signs mean emergency eye care or breathing care?

### Response metrics to track

- eye cloudiness
- squinting
- eye pain behavior
- pupil changes
- vision behavior
- breathing effort
- coughing
- appetite
- energy
- water intake and urination

## Griffey packet template

### One-sentence concern

Griffey has facial scabs, and because another animal may have similar signs, I want to sort contagious, parasitic, infectious, allergic, or shared-environment causes.

### Top question

Can we start with the most useful skin-pathway checks, such as flea combing, skin scraping, cytology, and ringworm testing if indicated?

### Specific questions

- Do the scabs look parasitic, infectious, allergic, traumatic, or fungal?
- Should skin scraping be done?
- Should cytology be done?
- Is ringworm testing indicated?
- Should vet-approved parasite treatment be used even if scraping is negative?
- Should bedding/tools be cleaned or animals separated while waiting?

### Response metrics to track

- number of scabs
- location
- itch level
- hair loss
- redness
- swelling
- discharge
- spread to other animals or people

## Nimby packet template

### One-sentence concern

Nimby has facial scabs or related skin signs, and I want to compare the pattern with Griffey to identify shared-environment, parasitic, infectious, or allergic causes.

### Top question

Does Nimby's pattern match Griffey's enough to treat this as a shared-environment or possible contagious skin problem?

### Specific questions

- Are Nimby's lesions the same pattern as Griffey's?
- Should both animals be evaluated together?
- What testing is most useful first?
- What response timeline should we expect after a vet-guided plan?

### Response metrics to track

- location match with Griffey
- spread over time
- itch level
- hair loss
- redness/swelling/discharge
- response after vet-guided intervention

## Phone-call version

Use this when overwhelmed:

I am calling because I need help deciding the highest-value next step. I have observations and a short packet. I am not asking for every test; I am asking which test or exam changes the decision most and what signs mean urgent care.

## Claim boundary

Allowed:

These templates help organize veterinary communication.

Not allowed:

These templates diagnose, prescribe, or replace veterinary care.
