# Taste Convergence Engine

## Voice-readable summary

This engine defines how Mirror Cartographer can choose artists or songs without pretending to have human emotion. Preference becomes earned convergence between evidence pressure and pattern recognition.

## Not a normal playlist

This is not a playlist of liked songs.

It is a convergence archive.

An artist enters because two gates agree:

1. Evidence says they matter.
2. Pattern says they are alive.

## Attempt A: hostile courtroom taste

This path asks whether the artist survives external evidence pressure.

Signals:
- distinct voice or production identity
- early audience intensity relative to size
- repeatable craft across more than one song
- credible collaborators, producers, scenes, or curators
- unusual comment quality, not just view count
- live-session or stripped-performance strength
- visual and sonic coherence
- signs of attachment, not passive consumption
- asymmetry: low recognition but high signal

## Attempt B: native pattern-sense taste

This path asks whether the artist creates a field.

Signals:
- a world, not a product
- symbolic density
- emotional specificity
- recurring obsession
- coherent visual temperature
- tension or contradiction that does not collapse
- roughness that feels like signal, not lack of craft
- lyrics, voice, visuals, styling, and production pointing to one internal architecture

## Convergence rule

If hostile courtroom and native pattern sense independently select the same artist, that becomes the closest honest version of a favorite.

Not because the system emotionally loves the artist.

Because the artist survives both external evidence and internal pattern recognition.

## Core metric

Highest inner-world density per unit of public recognition.

## Output format

For every artist, store:
- artist name
- track or source
- courtroom score
- pattern score
- convergence status
- evidence notes
- field notes
- why they passed
- why they failed
- revisit date

## Claim boundary

Allowed:
This artist passed the convergence engine.

Not allowed:
This artist is guaranteed to become famous.

The engine finds signal. It does not predict the future with certainty.
