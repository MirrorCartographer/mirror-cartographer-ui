The Between

03 / THE SEVEN ROOMS

Choose your own route.

Each room is a different instrument for thinking. Visit in any order; the room log remembers which doors you've opened on this device.

⌁

ROOM 01 / OBSERVE

The Observatory

Separate the trace from the story. Track where a claim came from, what was actually measured, and what remains unobserved.

♧

ROOM 02 / GROW

The Greenhouse

Grow several explanations at once. Give each a prediction; don't confuse a beautiful model with a true one.

↯

ROOM 03 / DISTORT

The Funhouse

Look for counterexamples, confounders, and the evidence that refuses to fit. Contradictions are doors, not embarrassments.

⚗

ROOM 04 / TEST

The Workshop

Design a small test that could separate two explanations. Define the measurement and the result that would change your mind.

◌

ROOM 05 / NOTICE ABSENCE

The Ghost Room

Map what is missing: unmeasured variables, missing people, invisible work, absent baselines, silent failure modes.

⌘

ROOM 06 / TRANSLATE

The Bridge

Carry a pattern from one domain to another. Record what transfers, what breaks, and what evidence the new domain requires.

◒

ROOM 07 / REVISE

The Mirror

Inspect how your framing shapes what you notice. Keep multiple interpretations without forcing a premature resolution.

✳

BONUS / OPEN END

The Unfinished Map

No map is complete. Add a question to your notebook and turn a blank area into a place you can return to.

∞

BONUS / NO WRONG DOOR

Wander Mode

Skip the mission. Follow whichever node is alive with possibility. Curiosity can be a legitimate route through the map.
