The design principle

MC is a translator and organizer, not an adjudicator. It can help a Veteran describe what they want, what happened, what they can do, what support changes their capacity, what resources may fit, and what happened after an action. It does not decide disability ratings, benefit eligibility, housing entitlement, compensation, diagnosis, legal outcomes or human worth.

For Veterans who are homeless or imminently at risk, VA's National Call Center for Homeless Veterans is 877-424-3838 and operates 24/7. VA describes its homeless system as combining housing with health, employment and wraparound support. Verify current eligibility and requirements with VA or an accredited representative.
