What actually
happens here?

A local field instrument for the living atlas. It counts visits, active time, route transitions, interaction attempts, obvious placeholder links, and browser errors. It does not record typed text, pointer coordinates, audio, or personal identity. Records stay in this browser on this site; nothing is transmitted.
