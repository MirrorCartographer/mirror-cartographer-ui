THE MIDNIGHT MILE

A carnival has arrived where the road should be. Five attractions are still lit. The gate has three teeth. Somewhere under the floorboards, a room is keeping time.

“You may leave with what you came for.”

The ticket clerk said this while looking at the thing in your hand. You had not noticed you were carrying anything.
