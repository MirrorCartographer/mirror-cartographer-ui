The self is not
a fixed object.

It is a moving system: body, attention, memory, environment, relationships, values, actions and time, continually affecting one another.

You do not have to explain yourself correctly. Start with one thing you notice. Place it somewhere. Let the map become more accurate as you live.

03 / Work with the pattern

Understanding is a cycle, not a verdict.

Observe

Keep the scene intact. What happened, when, where, with whom, and under what conditions? Separate what you noticed from what you think it means.

Compare

Look for recurrence and difference. The same response in different settings may point to different mechanisms. Different responses in the same setting may reveal a changed condition.

Vary

Change one low-risk condition when possible: timing, light, sequence, environment, support, or method. Notice what changes. Do not turn an experiment into a demand.

Revise

Keep what survives observation. Rename what no longer fits. Leave uncertainty visible. You are allowed to outgrow your own map.

To interact with yourself is to become both participant and careful observer—without treating the observer as the owner of the person.
