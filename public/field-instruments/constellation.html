The Unfinished Constellation

Some things only become visible when two different kinds of mind keep turning them over.

A human brings a life, a taste, a thousand strange connections, and the right to say no, not that. A language model brings synthesis, pattern search, structure, and an inexhaustible supply of first drafts. The interesting object is neither one alone. It is the trace left by their collision.

No account. No tracking. No external libraries. Your writing stays here.
