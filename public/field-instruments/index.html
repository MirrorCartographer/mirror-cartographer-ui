03 / E

Missing Evidence Engine

Investigate a small fictional case. The challenge is not to guess the answer; it is to identify what is missing, what each explanation predicts, and which next observation would separate them.

Case file / synthetic
Fictional scenario

The intermittent shutdown

A device shuts down during high demand. The log shows temperature rising before two shutdowns, but one shutdown has no temperature record. A replacement component was installed halfway through the observation period.

Investigation output

Keep hypotheses alive until evidence separates them.

Choose a gap, name competing explanations, and specify a discriminating observation.

Observation ≠ interpretation. Correlation ≠ cause. Missing data ≠ negative evidence. A changed system may not be comparable to the earlier system.
