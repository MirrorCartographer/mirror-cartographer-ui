How to read the loom

Confidence becomes a number

Each vessel has a known base rate, represented by its tokens. The draw is simulated in this browser using that rate. Your chosen probability is scored against the outcome that occurred.

0–1

Brier score
Lower is better across scored forecasts. Zero is a perfect forecast for that outcome; one is the largest error.

Important: a low score in one short run does not prove good calibration, and a high score does not prove bad judgment. Chance matters.

Calibration is assessed over many forecasts: for example, events assigned 70% probability should occur about 70% of the time across a sufficiently large set of comparable forecasts.
