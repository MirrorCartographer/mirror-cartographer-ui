Reveal the toy rule

In this deliberately simple model, the seed opens when light is at least 1 and water is at least 1. Wind has no effect. This is code in the demo, not a claim about real plants or human systems.

Evidence boundary. A one-variable comparison helps isolate a difference in this toy model. It does not establish a universal cause, explain a real person's behavior, or prove that the same rule applies outside this little world.
