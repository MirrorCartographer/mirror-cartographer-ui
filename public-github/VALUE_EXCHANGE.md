# Value Exchange

Mirror Cartographer can support public work through direct projects, sponsorship, collaboration, and barter.

## What can be offered

- archive organization
- project continuity mapping
- research/evidence mapping
- symbolic identity mapping
- public concept writing
- interface concept design
- record organization templates
- redacted demo artifacts

## What can be exchanged

- payment
- sponsorship
- technical help
- design help
- research review
- introductions
- public feedback
- hosting support
- collaboration time

## Boundary

Mirror Cartographer offers organization, structure, mapping, and public-safe artifacts.

It does not offer diagnosis, treatment, legal advice, financial guarantees, miracle claims, or private-record publication.

## First public offer

Bring one fragmented project, archive, record set, or symbolic system.

Mirror Cartographer returns a continuity map:

- what is known
- what is missing
- what connects
- what is uncertain
- what should be separated
- what could be built next
- what public artifact could invite the right conversation
