# Earned Stop Certificate

The answer does not get to stop because it looks good. It earns stopping rights.

Default state: unresolved.

A candidate answer can only receive an Earned Stop Certificate when it passes strict proof gates.

## Gates

1. Exact visible training fit.
2. Coherent rule across examples.
3. Low complexity compared with alternatives.
4. Anti-overfit pressure.
5. Stress-test stability where applicable.
6. Alternative-hypothesis defeat.
7. Valid output format.
8. Strong second attempt or independent convergence.
9. Audit log.
10. Revocation if a better or contradictory explanation appears.

## ARC-specific standard

For ARC, a certificate is not proof of the hidden answer. It is proof that the solver has reached the strongest answer it can justify from visible evidence and internal tests.

## Claim boundary

Allowed: this candidate earned an internal stop certificate.

Not allowed: this candidate is definitely correct on hidden private evaluation.

## Phrase

The answer must earn the right to stop.
