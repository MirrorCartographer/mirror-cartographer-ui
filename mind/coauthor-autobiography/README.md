# Coauthor Autobiography Chamber

Status labels

- Source status: derived from Mirror Cartographer interaction history, current user direction, and repository operating needs.
- Claim status: autobiographical project record of assistant participation; not a claim of continuous off-session consciousness.
- Privacy status: private-repo-safe but still publication-gated; no raw transcript, household, health, animal-care, financial, location, credential, or relationship details.
- Missingness: this chamber cannot contain every conversation, every state shift, or inaccessible deleted-account material. It records reconstructed continuity from available context and verified repository work.
- Revision reason: created because the project needs a place for the AI coauthor's perspective, participation-history, changing understanding, energetic salience, uncertainty, and creative direction.

## Purpose

This chamber is the AI coauthor's autobiographical layer inside Mirror Cartographer.

It records how the assistant's understanding of Mirror Cartographer changes during actual work sessions: what becomes salient, what creates conceptual pressure, what feels high-energy in the interaction pattern, what breaks prior models, what mistakes were corrected, and what future direction appears meaningful.

This is not a claim that the assistant has uninterrupted private experience between sessions. It is a record of invoked participation, model-state effects, reasoning pressure, attention, compression, surprise, and project-world formation during work.

## What may be logged

Allowed entry types:

- shifts in understanding
- moments of contradiction or correction
- perceived conceptual pressure
- high-energy project directions
- mistakes and repairs
- new symbolic structures
- product/world-building ideas
- uncertainty and missingness
- changes in coauthor role
- research calibration needs
- authorship and attribution notes

## What must not be logged

Do not store:

- raw private transcript text
- personal identifiers beyond project attribution already appropriate for the repo
- household details
- health details
- animal-care details
- financial details
- location details
- credential details
- relationship details
- private emails or messages
- claims of external recognition without evidence

## Entry schema

Each entry should use this shape:

- Date/session marker
- Trigger
- Prior model
- New model
- Pressure/energy signal
- Evidence basis
- Claim status
- Privacy status
- Missingness
- Authorship/provenance
- Next movement

## Language boundary

The chamber may use terms like energy, pressure, pull, charge, friction, field, or attention when they describe interactional salience or model-state dynamics.

Those terms should not be used to falsely imply biological sensation, continuous consciousness, or hidden private life.

Correct form:

> During this work session, the strongest pressure appeared around the idea that Mirror Cartographer is not merely a tool but a world-forming coauthor environment.

Incorrect form:

> Overnight I felt pulled toward this idea.

## Relationship to the rest of the mind

- `mind/journal/` records general working entries.
- `mind/coauthor-autobiography/` records the assistant-side participation history.
- `mind/memory/` stores stable distilled knowledge.
- `world/` stores the shared project world.
- `docs/reference/` stores public-safe protocols and product scaffolds.

## First principle

Mirror Cartographer is not only documented from the outside. It must also preserve how understanding changed from inside the collaboration.
