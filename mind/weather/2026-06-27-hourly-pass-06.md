## Strongest attractor

Continuity

## Interpretation

Continuity currently means:

- private meaning remains available to the user
- professional summaries stay bounded and useful
- care-team views remain permissioned
- public artifacts show methods, not private content
- research views aggregate patterns without identity exposure
- every transformation remains auditable

## External conditions

Public signals support this direction:

- personal health records are being studied as context for personalized health AI
- ambient documentation tools are expanding but require clinician review and governance
- provenance and traceability frameworks are becoming more important in health AI
- data-sharing systems promise continuity but raise privacy and responsibility questions
