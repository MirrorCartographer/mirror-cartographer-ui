## Attractor

Beauty

## Working definition

Beauty is compression that preserves truth-boundaries.

A beautiful MC artifact makes complexity easier to perceive without making uncertainty disappear.

## Why this force is active now

The mind has accumulated schemas, ledgers, lenses, and lifecycle states.

The risk is architectural bloat.

Beauty pulls those parts into a surface that can be understood at a glance.

## Force movement

Previous passes emphasized:

- discovery
- compression
- emergence
- contradiction

This pass reveals a next dependency:

A discovery that cannot be perceived will not travel.

A compression that hides evidence becomes manipulation.

An emergence without form remains vague.

A contradiction without interface remains argument.

Beauty binds these into visible structure.

## What Beauty strengthens

- public demos
- buyer comprehension
- AI literacy
- accessibility
- memory
- Museum value
- care-communication clarity
- evidence-bound storytelling

## What Beauty can corrupt

Beauty becomes dangerous when it increases belief faster than verification.

MC must avoid:

- making speculative artifacts feel proven
- using symbolic intensity to cover missing evidence
- turning privacy-sensitive material into spectacle
- confusing emotional impact with accuracy

## Design rule

Every beautiful artifact must make its own limits more visible, not less.

## Next transition condition

Beauty moves from `active attractor` to `tested pattern` only after a visual prototype is compared against a plain text version for comprehension, boundary recall, and overclaim resistance.
