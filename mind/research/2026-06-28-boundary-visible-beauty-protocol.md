## Revision reason
Previous runs emphasized continuity packets, ViewDiff, consent gates, and care adapters. This pass compresses those into an interface rule: the user should be able to see what kind of statement they are looking at before believing it.

## Protocol
Boundary-visible beauty means the interface makes the following distinctions visually legible:

1. Source boundary: where the statement came from.
2. Claim boundary: whether it is observation, interpretation, hypothesis, question, recommendation, or fiction.
3. Privacy boundary: who may see it.
4. Transformation boundary: what changed from the private source to the current view.
5. Missingness boundary: what is unknown, unverified, or deliberately withheld.
6. Review boundary: whether a human/professional has checked it.
7. Action boundary: whether the next step is reflection, documentation, discussion, or professional review.

## Design rule
If the beautiful version makes a weak claim feel stronger than it is, the design failed.

If the beautiful version helps the viewer understand uncertainty faster, the design is doing MC work.

## Implementation plan
Build a fictional card demo with four visible bands:

- Origin band: source type and privacy status.
- Meaning band: symbolic or narrative interpretation.
- Evidence band: observation/question/missingness labels.
- ViewDiff band: what was transformed, removed, generalized, or preserved.

## Evaluation criteria
- Can a viewer tell whether a statement is observed, inferred, or speculative?
- Can a viewer tell whether the packet is private, care-team, public, or research-safe?
- Can a viewer tell what was removed or generalized?
- Can a viewer identify the next safest action?
- Does the design reduce confusion without flattening meaning?

## Research questions
- Do visual claim-boundary labels improve AI literacy compared with text-only disclosures?
- Can boundary-visible design reduce overreliance on AI-generated summaries?
- Can consent-aware view design improve comfort with sensitive observation sharing?
- What is the smallest visual notation that still preserves source, claim, privacy, missingness, and revision state?

## Key phrase
Beauty is not the polish over the boundary. Beauty is the boundary becoming visible.
