## Strongest attractor for this run

Discovery.

Not discovery as a mood.

Discovery as a process with traceable stages:

1. unresolved material enters the system.
2. the system rotates it across domains.
3. a non-obvious structural match appears.
4. the match becomes a hypothesis.
5. the hypothesis is tested against sources, constraints, and alternatives.
6. the surviving result changes the map.

## Fresh research signal

Current AI provenance work is moving toward multi-layer verification: metadata, watermarking, perceptual fingerprints, and architectural provenance records.

Recent AI transparency research argues that disclosure cannot be reduced to post-hoc labels; interleaved human-AI work requires architectural transparency and machine-readable provenance.

Recent human-AI co-creativity research identifies transparency, user control, externalization of thought, early-stage problem clarification, and adaptive proactive behavior as major design concerns.

Recent creativity measurement work argues that in AI-mediated environments, distinctiveness and transformation process matter more than fluent surface quality.

## MC interpretation

MC's public-safe opportunity is not merely to label outputs as AI-assisted.

The stronger opportunity is to preserve how an idea changed while moving through human intuition, symbolic language, source boundaries, domain translations, evidence checks, and public-safe release gates.

This suggests the primitive unit should not be the finished artifact.

The primitive unit should be the transformation trace.

## Discovery Provenance Prism

A `DiscoveryProvenancePrism` is a structured record of how one idea changes when rotated across domains and tested.

Required fields:

- seed statement
- invariant structure
- domain translations
- source boundary
- claim boundary
- privacy boundary
- blocked or revised wording, if any
- contrast between original and public-safe form
- test method
- outcome
- map change
