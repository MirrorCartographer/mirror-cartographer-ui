## Exhibit thesis

A meaning can survive while the words around it change.

But it does not survive automatically.

It survives only if the system preserves the invariant structure and records what each translation adds or removes.

## The preserved transformation

Earlier MC framing:

`Preserve the transformation trace.`

New compression layer:

`Preserve the invariant beneath the transformation trace.`

Combined form:

`Track how meaning moves without pretending every wording means the same thing.`

## Why this belongs in the Museum

Before this pass, MC had many rooms and vocabularies.

`A system is held by a repeated pull.`

Translations:

- physics: attractor

Meaning gained by translation:

Each field reveals a different action possibility.

Meaning lost by translation:

Each field can smuggle in assumptions that do not belong.

Museum label:

The words changed.

The pull remained.
