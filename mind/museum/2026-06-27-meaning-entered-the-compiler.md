The public language was safe.

Compile the claim.

Do not publish the private source.

Do not bleach the meaning until nothing remains.

Instead, show the crossing:

- source boundary
- claim boundary
- privacy boundary
- missingness boundary
- revision boundary

The artifact became less like a confession and more like a vessel.

Not everything entered.

But what entered could be carried.
