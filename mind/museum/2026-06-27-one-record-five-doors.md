## Exhibit title

One Record, Five Doors

## What became museum-worthy

The project discovered that the private/public distinction is too simple.

The better structure is:

one underlying record

moving through

five audience-specific doors

with

visible transformation trace.

## The five doors

1. Private self-understanding
2. Professional handoff
3. Care-team coordination
4. Public-safe method sharing
5. Research-safe aggregation

## Why it matters

The same meaning does not need to be exposed the same way to everyone.

A record can remain continuous without becoming universally visible.

This makes privacy, usefulness, and discovery compatible instead of enemies.
