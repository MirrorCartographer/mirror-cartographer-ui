## Exhibit title

One Record, Five Doors

## What became museum-worthy

The project discovered that the private/public distinction is too simple.

The better structure is:

one underlying continuity record

moving through

purpose-limited permissioned doors

with

visible transformation trace.

## The five doors

1. Private meaning view.
2. Self-review view.
3. Professional handoff view.
4. Care-team continuity view.
5. Public-safe method view.

A sixth research-safe aggregate view can exist only when de-identification and aggregation are strong enough to avoid exposing individual records.

## Why it matters

The same meaning does not need to be exposed the same way to everyone.

A record can remain continuous without becoming universally visible.

This makes privacy, usefulness, and discovery compatible instead of enemies.

## What must be preserved

The door is not the record.

The view is not the whole person.

The summary is not the source.

The handoff is not the diagnosis.

The research aggregate is not the individual.
