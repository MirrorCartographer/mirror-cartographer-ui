## Turn

The project moved from:

`private version + professional version`

to:

`one continuity record + many permissioned views + visible transformation trace`

## Why this matters

A binary private/shared model cannot handle MC's actual structure.

The same underlying record may need to become:

- private reflection
- self-review
- professional handoff
- care-team continuity
- public-safe method
- research-safe aggregate

Each version must carry different boundaries.
