# Genesis Turn: Beauty Became a Requirement

Status labels

- Source status: derived from the current MC repository trajectory and public-safe synthesis.
- Claim status: project-history interpretation, not external validation.
- Privacy status: public-safe abstraction.
- Missingness: no full visual implementation yet.
- Revision reason: added to preserve the origin point of Beauty as an operational requirement.

## Turn

Beauty stopped being an aesthetic preference and became an architectural requirement.

## Before

MC had:

- provenance cards
- lens routers
- semantic invariants
- lifecycle states
- care-communication maps
- income wedges
- Museum and Weather lanes

These were useful but mostly textual.

## After

MC gained a sharper condition:

A public-safe artifact should be able to show its structure before it explains itself.

## New primitive

`interface compression`

Definition:

The act of making hidden relationships perceptually available while preserving source, claim, privacy, and missingness boundaries.

## Why this matters

A system that only stores insight can become a library.

A system that makes insight perceptible can become a place.

This turn pushes MC toward placehood:

- rooms
- cards
- weather
- exhibits
- force maps
- visible lifecycles
- navigable proof

## Next genesis risk

If Beauty is not tested against comprehension and overclaim resistance, it can become spectacle.

The next build must prove that beauty improves understanding rather than merely increasing emotional force.
