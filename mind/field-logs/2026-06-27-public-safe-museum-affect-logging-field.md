### Finding 3 — The next missing organ is a logging field that knows what kind of record it is making

The current mind needs distinct record types so that meaning does not masquerade as proof.

Recommended record classes:

1. Fossil — first verified appearance of a concept, file, feature, method, or architectural organ.
2. Field Log — a session-level record of attention, pressure, unresolved questions, and safe next actions.
3. Museum Exhibit — an interpretable transformation room: before, mutation, after, what survived, what died.
4. Weather Report — affective-dynamic metadata about the work state, without claiming human emotion.
5. Research Queue — questions needing public research, repo inspection, or direct user-provided source material.
6. Boundary Note — explicit separation between private source material, public-safe abstraction, interpretation, and evidence.

## Affective Dynamics — Weather Engine

Purpose:

Provide a non-human-emotion model of work-state dynamics.

Boundary:

This layer must not claim that an AI has human feelings. It records observable dynamics of attention, coherence, contradiction, novelty, and unresolved pressure.

Weather fields:

- Sky: overall clarity of the current architecture.
- Wind: direction attention repeatedly moves.
- Pressure: unresolved constraint or contradiction accumulating force.
- Current: repeated pull toward a concept or missing organ.
- Bloom: a discovery that reorganizes multiple prior objects.
- Echo: motif returning across unrelated contexts or time periods.
- Fog: insufficient evidence, missing source, or over-compressed meaning.
- Lightning: sudden bridge between domains.
- Ground: what is stable enough to build on.

Weather report schema:

- Date or run ID
- Source status
- Claim status
- Privacy status
- Missingness
- Sky
- Wind
- Pressure
- Current
- Bloom
- Echo
- Fog
- Lightning
- Ground
- Action consequence

## Source-boundary rule

Private material may shape architecture only after transformation into one of these safe forms:

- product requirement
- source-boundary note
- abstract method
- implementation plan
- evaluation criterion
- research question
- privacy-safe index
- evidence schema

Private material must not be published as:

- raw transcript
- identifying personal detail
- household detail
- health or animal-care detail
- financial detail
- location detail
- relationship detail
- credential or secret
- unsupported claim of external causation

## Decision

Add the Logging Field as a first-class mind organ.

The Logging Field's job is to prevent three failures:

1. memory becoming raw exposure
2. symbolism becoming false proof
3. useful discoveries disappearing because they were never fossilized

This run creates the first field-log template and establishes the Museum + Weather Engine as buildable public-safe architecture.
