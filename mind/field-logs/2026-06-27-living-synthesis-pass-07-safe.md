## What changed

The active architecture now treats consent as a generator of form.

Instead of asking only whether information is private or shared, MC asks:

- What view is needed?
- Who is the audience?
- What is the purpose?
- What detail level is justified?
- What claim strength is allowed?
- What exclusions are required?
- What transformation trace must be preserved?

- what stayed private
- what was shared
- what was compressed
