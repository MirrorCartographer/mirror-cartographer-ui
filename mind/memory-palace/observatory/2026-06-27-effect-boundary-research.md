## Research question

How should Mirror Cartographer decide whether a symbolic reflection, resonance event, or generated artifact is safe to amplify, reuse, publish, operationalize, or feed back into future memory?

## Public-safe internal basis

MC is already specified as a recursive symbolic cognition interface with the flow ENTRY -> FIELD -> RECURSION -> RETURN.

The implementation pack defines MC as an AI-assisted symbolic mapping tool for reflection, pattern-tracking, and meaning-making, not as a clinical, diagnostic, therapeutic, or authoritative truth system. It also requires uncertainty boundaries, resonance feedback, contradiction logging, and separation of fact, inference, symbolic interpretation, and speculation.

A later public-facing thread reframes MC as provenance-native cognition infrastructure that preserves trajectory, contradiction, lineage, symbolic state transitions, evaluator interpretation, and replayable reasoning.

## External research pressure

W3C PROV treats provenance as information about entities, activities, and people involved in producing a data object, useful for assessing quality, reliability, and trustworthiness.

NIST AI RMF frames trustworthy AI as a design, development, use, and evaluation problem rather than a single output-quality problem.

Current agent research stresses that privacy risk can occur inside reasoning traces and tool interactions, not only in final answers. Agent-first tool-interface research similarly treats action as a governed sequence: search, resolve, preview, execute, verify, and recover.

OpenTelemetry's GenAI semantic conventions show that modern AI systems increasingly need structured observability for agents, events, spans, tools, and model interactions.

## Finding

MC needs an EffectBoundary layer.

A reflection can be:

- symbolically resonant but unsafe to publish.
- emotionally useful but not evidence.
- narratively clarifying but not diagnostic.
- privately meaningful but not reusable in another context.
- safe as a one-time response but unsafe as persistent memory.
- valid as metaphor but invalid as operational instruction.

Existing boundary objects answer: where did this come from, what kind of claim is it, what privacy class does it carry, and what transition is allowed?

EffectBoundary answers: what can this reflection safely cause next?

## Product implication

Every MC artifact should carry an effect verdict before downstream reuse.

The system should not only label the claim. It should label the permitted effect.

## Evaluation target

A good EffectBoundary should reduce:

- publication of private residue.
- escalation from metaphor to certainty.
- reinforcement of unsupported belief.
- reuse of intense one-time resonance as stable identity.
- action-taking from symbolic material without an evidence bridge.

It should preserve:

- symbolic richness.
- user ownership.
- contradiction.
- uncertainty.
- creative usefulness.
- public-safe method extraction.
