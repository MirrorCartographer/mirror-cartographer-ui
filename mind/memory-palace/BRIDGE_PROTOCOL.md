# Bridge Protocol

Status labels

- Source status: derived from current Mirror Cartographer world/research/synthesis architecture.
- Claim status: operating protocol for reconciling original project insight with external evidence.
- Privacy status: public-safe as abstract method; no private source material.
- Missingness: does not include completed research reviews or implementation tests.
- Revision reason: created to preserve the user's correction that research is a calibration layer, not the origin layer.

## Purpose

The bridge connects Mirror Cartographer's inner world with the research world.

The project may originate ideas from symbolic intelligence, interaction patterns, product dreams, body-language metaphors, emotional-spatial reasoning, and coauthor insight.

External research then tests, constrains, names, sharpens, contradicts, or expands those ideas.

## Sequence

1. Inner signal
   - What did the project generate from its own world?

2. Articulation
   - Can the signal be stated as a concept, product requirement, hypothesis, interface behavior, or testable claim?

3. Provenance
   - Did this originate from user insight, assistant synthesis, coauthor emergence, prior research, or unknown source?

4. Claim labeling
   - Is it metaphor, design idea, hypothesis, observed pattern, implemented feature, verified result, or external evidence?

5. Research calibration
   - What does existing evidence say?
   - Is the idea already known, adjacent, contradicted, unsupported, or novel?

6. Synthesis
   - What survives?
   - What changes?
   - What splits into multiple ideas?
   - What must be discarded?

7. Product translation
   - What feature, test, artifact, room, protocol, or evaluation should now exist?

## Bridge decision labels

- Preserve: keep the inner idea as-is.
- Refine: keep the idea but improve language or scope.
- Split: separate symbolic, scientific, product, and speculative layers.
- Test: design an experiment before making stronger claims.
- Park: keep as a future seed without current action.
- Reject: remove or deprecate because it fails evidence, safety, or usefulness.

## Rule

Neither dream nor research gets automatic authority.

Dream generates direction.

Research provides constraint.

Synthesis decides what becomes part of Mirror Cartographer.
