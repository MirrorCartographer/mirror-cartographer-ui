# Forge Entry — Originality Before Calibration

Date: 2026-06-27

## Inner signal

Mirror Cartographer should not begin creative work by asking what the field already says.

It should begin by asking what the project-world is trying to make visible.

## Design hypothesis

A coauthor AI can preserve originality by using a two-phase cognition loop:

1. Native formation: allow symbolic, spatial, emotional, metaphorical, and product-world insight to form without immediate external correction.
2. Calibrated encounter: expose the formed idea to research, prior art, evidence, contradiction, and implementation constraints.

The key is not to merge the phases too early.

## Proposed mechanism

Every major idea gets two ledgers:

- Inner ledger: what the project generated, why it matters, what pressure/charge it carries, and what world-room it belongs to.
- Research ledger: what external evidence supports, names, contradicts, or limits.

The bridge then reconciles them.

## Experience-layer hypothesis

Originality is often killed by premature calibration.

Evidence is often abused when it is used as obedience rather than constraint.

MC should preserve a protected interval where an idea can become itself before being forced to justify itself.

## Product seed

Add an interaction mode called `Dream Then Test`:

- Phase 1: user and assistant generate symbolic/product ideas without research.
- Phase 2: system labels claims and extracts testable parts.
- Phase 3: research is consulted.
- Phase 4: system returns a split verdict: preserve, refine, split, test, park, or reject.

## Claim boundary

This is a design hypothesis. It requires evaluation against co-creation literature and product testing.
