# Bridge Room

Status labels

- Source status: derived from memory-palace bridge protocol.
- Claim status: synthesis-routing scaffold.
- Privacy status: public-safe as abstract structure.
- Missingness: specific bridge runs are separate entries.
- Revision reason: created to keep inner-world invention and research-world evidence in tension without either dominating.

## Function

The bridge compares forge output with observatory evidence.

It asks:

- What did MC originate internally?
- What does research say?
- Where do they align?
- Where do they conflict?
- What needs testing?
- What should become product behavior?

## Rule

Do not let imagination become proof.

Do not let research become obedience.

Synthesis must preserve the value of both.
