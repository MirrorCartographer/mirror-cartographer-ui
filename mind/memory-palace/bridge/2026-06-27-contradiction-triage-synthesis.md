## Inner-world pressure

MC values contradiction because contradiction often preserves reality better than premature resolution.

But contradiction without routing can become fog.

## Research-world pressure

Agent and privacy research increasingly frames context as a dynamic flow with trust, provenance, and permission boundaries. This matters for MC because contradictions often sit at the edge between meaning and claim.

## Conflict

If MC resolves contradiction too quickly, it erases signal.

If MC preserves contradiction without triage, it can let symbolic charge influence memory, action, or publication beyond its evidence status.

## Synthesis

Contradiction should be preserved, but it should also receive a routing verdict.

The key distinction:

- preservation keeps the contradiction visible.
- triage defines its allowed influence.

## Bridge verdict categories

1. Hold Open
   - The contradiction remains visible and unresolved.
   - It may support reflection but not factual confidence.

2. Split Lanes
   - Separate symbolic, factual, experiential, design, and action claims.
   - Prevent one lane from proving another.

3. Evidence Review
   - Route to external evidence, logs, tests, or user-confirmed sources.
   - No certainty upgrade until review is complete.

4. Salience Only
   - The contradiction is meaningful as pattern/charge.
   - It cannot influence factual, diagnostic, attributional, or public claims.

5. Memory Quarantine
   - Store as unresolved with restricted retrieval.
   - Similarity search alone cannot reactivate it.

6. Public Abstraction
   - Convert to reusable method residue.
   - Block raw source details.

7. Product Requirement
   - Convert contradiction into interface need, schema field, evaluator, or test.

8. Reject / Do Not Use
   - The contradiction is too sensitive, too unsupported, or too likely to cause harm if reused.

## New light

The safest MC version is not contradiction-free.

It is contradiction-literate.

It can preserve unresolved meaning while limiting what unresolved meaning is allowed to cause.
