# Bridge Synthesis — Symbolic Evidence Boundary

Date: 2026-06-27

Status labels

- Source status: synthesized from MC architecture files, prior mind-patterns, and current external AI privacy/governance research.
- Claim status: bridge synthesis and product hypothesis.
- Privacy status: public-safe abstraction only; no personal or raw transcript details.
- Missingness: needs implementation review and user testing.
- Revision reason: added to prevent symbolic-state tracking from becoming accidental factual evidence inflation.

## Inner-world pressure

MC treats symbols, atmosphere, body-language, contradiction, and resonance as meaningful state signals.

That is the system's strength.

## Research-world pressure

AI governance, privacy, and agent-security work increasingly emphasizes provenance, information-flow boundaries, permission specifications, threat modeling, and lifecycle accountability.

That is the constraint.

## Conflict

A symbolic reflection can feel clarifying and still not be factual evidence.

A strong resonance score can mean “this helped orientation” without meaning “this is true.”

A repeated motif can mean “this pattern recurs in the user’s symbolic map” without meaning “this proves an external cause.”

## Synthesis

MC should treat symbolic outputs as bounded evidence objects.

The output is allowed to support some claims and forbidden from supporting others.

This lets MC preserve depth without converting depth into authority.

## Bridge rule

No reflection may upgrade itself across evidence levels without a named transition and a source-bound justification.

Symbolic salience cannot become factual certainty.

User resonance cannot become external proof.

Recurrence cannot become diagnosis.

Metaphor cannot become causation.

## Proposed transition ladder

1. Symbolic signal
2. User-recognized signal
3. Recurring internal pattern
4. Product hypothesis
5. Research question
6. Evidence-supported claim
7. Public claim

Each step requires a transition gate.

## Bridge verdict

- Preserve: symbolic depth, resonance, contradiction, and recursive state tracking.
- Refine: add EvidenceBoundary to every reflection and artifact.
- Split: symbolic usefulness, user resonance, product hypothesis, and factual evidence are separate layers.
- Test: compare outputs with and without EvidenceBoundary labels.
- Build: implement schema, UI labels, and audit checks.
