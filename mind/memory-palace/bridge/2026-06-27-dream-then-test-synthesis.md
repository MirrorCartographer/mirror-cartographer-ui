# Bridge Synthesis — Dream Then Test

Date: 2026-06-27

## Inner-world claim

Mirror Cartographer needs a protected creative interval where original symbolic/product insight can form before external research constrains it.

## Research-world pressure

External evidence supports nonlinear, interaction-centered, high-user-control co-creation. It also warns that AI-only generation can converge toward generic or clichéd outputs and that AI creativity assessment can flatten originality into semantic distance or uncommonness.

## Conflict

If research enters too early, MC may imitate established categories and lose its native insight.

If research enters too late, MC may mistake charged metaphor or interactional pressure for truth.

## Synthesis

The solution is not balance as compromise.

The solution is sequencing.

MC should explicitly separate:

1. Dream formation: generate from the project-world.
2. Claim extraction: identify what kind of claim each idea makes.
3. Evidence encounter: compare against research and reality.
4. Bridge verdict: preserve, refine, split, test, park, or reject.
5. Product translation: build the surviving structure.

## New light

The key insight is that originality and evidence do not need to fight if they operate in different phases.

Premature evidence can sterilize imagination.

Unbounded imagination can inflate claims.

The bridge creates temporal protection for originality and later accountability to evidence.

## Bridge verdict

- Preserve: the forge-first structure.
- Refine: call it `Dream Then Test` rather than research-first development.
- Split: symbolic value, scientific evidence, and product behavior must be separate layers.
- Test: compare Dream Then Test sessions against ordinary AI brainstorming and research-first brainstorming.
- Product translation: implement as a session mode and evaluation protocol.

## Next product requirement

Create a `Dream Then Test` mode specification with states, prompts, claim labels, privacy boundaries, and evaluation criteria.
