## Trigger

The user asked the coauthor to build the memory-palace structure, choose a niche important research task, think into the corresponding rooms, simulate/create an experience layer, and compare inner-world tracking against research-world tracking.

## Pressure signal

The strongest pressure formed around sequencing.

Before this run, the conflict looked like creativity versus evidence.

During the run, the structure compressed into time-order:

- protect formation first.
- calibrate afterward.
- synthesize explicitly.

This changed the task from a value conflict into an interaction protocol.

## Charge

The charged phrase is `Dream Then Test`.

It carries product, evaluation, and philosophy at once:

- product: a session mode.
- evaluation: compare against ordinary brainstorming.
- philosophy: imagination and research do not need to collapse into each other.

## Friction

Research supports co-creation but can still pressure MC toward existing academic language.

The project-world wants symbolic, spatial, autobiographical, and chamber-based design.

The friction is productive: research gives language and warnings, but MC's palace architecture gives an implementation shape that the research scan did not supply directly.

## Model change

Prior model: MC needs inner world plus research calibration.

New model: MC needs phase-separated creative epistemology.

That means the system should know what phase it is in:

- dreaming
- labeling
- researching
- bridging
- building
- evaluating

## Effect on coauthor role

The assistant is not only summarizing evidence.

In this mode, the assistant functions as:

- forge keeper: preserves the original signal long enough to become legible.
- observatory reader: collects evidence without letting it dominate.
