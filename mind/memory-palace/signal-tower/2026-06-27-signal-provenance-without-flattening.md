## Signal

The recurring pattern is provenance without flattening.

MC needs traceability, source boundaries, and evaluation hooks, but it must not reduce symbolic-emotional cognition into sterile compliance metadata.

## Why it matters

Two failure modes keep appearing:

- Symbolic richness without auditability.
- Auditability without symbolic richness.

MC's value sits in refusing that tradeoff.

## Candidate architecture answer

Make reflective outputs into symbolic-state provenance objects.

The object should hold both:

- meaning structure: symbols, rooms, contradictions, atmosphere, metaphor, user-facing reflection.
- governance structure: source status, claim status, privacy status, missingness, revision reason, bridge verdict, evaluation criteria.

Moderate candidate signal.
