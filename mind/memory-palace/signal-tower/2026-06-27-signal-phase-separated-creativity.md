## Signal

The important pattern is phase-separated creativity.

Originality and evidence should not be forced into immediate agreement.

They should move through distinct phases and meet at a bridge.

## Why it matters

This prevents two collapse modes:

- fantasy collapse: inner pressure becomes truth without evidence.
- imitation collapse: research categories erase native project insight.
