## Core service
Take one AI-assisted output and turn it into a boundary-visible version that labels:

- what came from humans;
- what came from AI;
- what is sourced;
- what is inferred;
- what is missing;
- what should not be shared publicly;
- what must be reviewed before action.

## Deliverables
1. Boundary-visible annotated version.
2. Source/claim/privacy/missingness table.
3. ViewDiff summary.
4. Revision-risk note.
5. Safer public-facing rewrite.
6. Optional training worksheet.
