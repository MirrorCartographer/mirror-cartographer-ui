- Revision reason: added as the persistent income lane for the discovery pass.

## Offer name

`Five Doors: How to Make AI Output Safe Enough to Use`

## Buyer problem

Teams are adopting AI faster than they can explain, verify, govern, or train around it.

They do not only need prompts.

They need a way to inspect:

- what the AI used
- what the AI claimed
- what the AI changed
- what the AI omitted
- what should remain private
- what needs human review
- what can be shared

## Public market signal

Current public reporting and research show:

- large organizations are training staff on ethical AI risks
- many businesses report AI skill shortages
- nonprofit and workforce programs are funding AI adoption and literacy
- freelancers and knowledge workers need credible ways to signal AI-assisted competencies
- enterprise AI governance research is moving toward traceability, context envelopes, and structured output contracts

## MC-specific wedge

Instead of selling generic AI training, MC can sell a compact demonstration:

`One messy AI output becomes five bounded views.`

The workshop/demo teaches people to build an output boundary ledger.

## Deliverable

A 60-90 minute remote session or recorded demo:

1. Start with a harmless fictional AI-generated note.
2. Identify source, claim, privacy, and missingness problems.
3. Route it into five views.
4. Show what changed between views.
5. Produce a reusable Output Boundary Card.

## Audience

Best early audiences:

- small nonprofits adopting AI
- solo operators using AI with client data
- coaches/consultants who need privacy-safe client summaries
- writers/researchers using AI for synthesis
- small clinical-adjacent or care-adjacent teams needing non-clinical communication hygiene

## Why this is realistic

The offer is small, explainable, and does not require a full software platform first.

It converts MC architecture into a teachable inspection method.

## First price hypothesis

- Recorded self-serve demo: low-cost entry product.
- Live small-group workshop: mid-price offer.
- Custom output-autopsy session: higher-price service.

No price should be treated as validated until tested.
