# Claim Boundary Audit Offer Safe Version

Status labels

- Source status: derived from GitHub mind direction, public-safe file-library summaries, and current public research on AI literacy, provenance, and human-AI co-creation.
- Claim status: business hypothesis / offer design; not revenue validation.
- Privacy status: public-safe; no private client data, personal finances, household details, health details, animal-care details, credentials, or raw transcripts included.
- Missingness: no landing page, no pricing test, no sample report, no customer interviews, no payment flow, no legal terms.
- Revision reason: an earlier draft was blocked or inappropriate because it mixed publication-audit language with care-adjacent examples. This safe version narrows the offer to non-clinical AI publication hygiene.

## Offer name

Claim Boundary Audit

## One-sentence offer

A lightweight review that marks what an AI-assisted artifact can safely claim, what it cannot claim yet, what sources it depends on, what is missing, and what should be rewritten before publication.

## Buyer problem

AI-generated documents often sound finished before their boundaries are visible.

Common failure modes:

- unsupported certainty
- hidden source gaps
- private context leaking into public wording
- metaphor presented as fact
- speculation presented as evidence
- missing limitations
- unclear human authorship and AI assistance

## Deliverable

A short annotated report with:

1. Source boundary table
2. Claim-strength table
3. Privacy-risk table
4. Missingness table
5. Public-safe rewrite notes
6. Recommended next test

## Starter package

- Input: one non-sensitive AI-assisted artifact.
- Output: public-safe boundary report.
- Positioning: AI literacy, publication hygiene, provenance training, and human-AI workflow safety.

## Realistic customer lanes

- creators using AI for public writing
- small teams publishing AI-assisted explainers
- educators teaching AI literacy
- nonprofits adopting AI tools
- research groups turning notes into public summaries
- product teams writing AI-generated documentation

## Boundaries

This is not:

- legal compliance review
- medical advice
- therapy advice
- privacy certification
- model safety certification
- guarantee of truth or safety

It is:

- a human-readable boundary audit
- a public-safe rewrite support process
- a provenance and claim-literacy artifact
