## Core finding

Do not try to sell the entire Mirror Cartographer world first.

Sell a narrow, legible capability that can be understood quickly.

## Highest-potential paid wedges

### 1. Dream Then Test workflow

Problem:

AI brainstorming often becomes generic, derivative, or overconfident.

MC capability:

Separate imagination, claim labeling, research, bridge verdict, and build plan.

Possible users:

- creative technologists
- product teams
- grant writers
- researchers
- founders
- artists using AI
- education or workshop settings

Demo:

Run the same prompt through ordinary AI brainstorming, research-first synthesis, and Dream Then Test. Show why MC produces more original but better-bounded output.

### 2. AI provenance and reflection audit

Problem:

Teams use AI outputs but cannot easily explain source boundaries, claim status, privacy risks, or what should not be reused.

MC capability:

EvidenceBoundary, TransitionGate, ContextReleaseProfile, PublicSafeAbstractionIndex, and SymbolicFlowLedger.

Possible users:

- AI product teams
- nonprofits adopting AI
- research groups
- responsible AI teams
- reflective software builders

Demo:

Take an AI-generated artifact and mark what is safe to publish, what is private-only, what is evidence, what is inference, and what actions are blocked.

### 3. Reflective information map

Problem:

People often have messy observations, metaphors, notes, and contradictions that ordinary documents do not organize well.

MC capability:

Turn unstructured reflection into a map with source labels, uncertainty, recurrence, privacy boundaries, and next questions.

Possible users:

- writers
- researchers
- educators
- coaches using non-clinical reflection tools
- creators organizing complex projects

Demo:

Convert a messy project journal into a one-page map with symbols, contradictions, open questions, and next actions.

### 4. Symbolic journaling prototype

Problem:

Most journaling tools store entries but do not help users navigate symbols, metaphors, emotional tone, contradictions, and changes over time.

MC capability:

Symbolic state mapping with privacy and evidence boundaries.

Possible users:

- reflective writing communities
- creative writers
- artists
- personal knowledge-management users

Demo:

A small interactive session that turns symbols and metaphors into a navigable map with claim boundaries.
