## Core finding

The most legible paid wedge is no longer only `Dream Then Test`.

The stronger package is:

`Emergent Provenance: Make AI-assisted thinking inspectable before it becomes an output.`

This still contains Dream Then Test, but it packages the buyer value more clearly.

## Buyer-readable problem

Organizations increasingly use AI during research, writing, ideation, education, training, and service design.

They often cannot show:

- where the idea came from.
- what changed during AI collaboration.
- which claims are evidence-backed.
- which claims are speculative.
- what private material was abstracted away.
- what wording changed because of safety, privacy, or audience needs.
- what test should happen next.

## MC demo promise

MC can demonstrate a workflow where one idea moves through:

1. imaginative generation.
2. invariant extraction.
3. domain rotation.
4. source and claim labeling.
5. privacy-safe transformation.
6. lifecycle state assignment.
7. practical next test.

## External market signal

Public signals supporting this wedge:

- AI transparency obligations and technical standards are moving toward dual human-readable and machine-readable labeling, but researchers argue compliance requires architecture, not just labels. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26983
- Human-AI co-creativity research identifies user control, transparency, externalized thought, and early problem clarification as important system design dimensions. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21333
- Current AI provenance efforts increasingly combine multiple mechanisms such as metadata credentials, watermarks, registries, and verification portals. Sources: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/933442/openai-synthid-content-credentials-c2pa-expansion and https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02412

## Why this is stronger than generic AI literacy

Generic AI literacy teaches tool use.

MC-style provenance literacy teaches meaning movement:

- what changed.
- why it changed.
- what boundary controlled the change.
- what remains unproven.
- what test comes next.
