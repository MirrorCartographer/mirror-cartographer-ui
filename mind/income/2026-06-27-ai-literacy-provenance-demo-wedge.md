## Wedge

`AI Literacy Through Provenance: How to Know What an AI Output Is Actually Made Of`

## Buyer/user problem

People and organizations use AI outputs but cannot easily answer:

- what came from the user
- what came from the model
- what came from sources
- what was inferred
- what is missing
- what changed during rewriting
- what is safe to publish

## MC capability

MC can package its source-boundary architecture as a teachable workflow:

1. input enters.
2. claims are labeled.
3. private material is abstracted.
4. domain translation is recorded.
5. public-safe output is produced.
6. the contrast is preserved.
