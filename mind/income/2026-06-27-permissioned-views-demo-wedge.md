# Permissioned Views Demo Wedge

Status labels

- Source status: derived from public-safe MC architecture, GitHub mind synthesis, and current public research on AI literacy, provenance, personal health records, and consent-bounded sharing.
- Claim status: practical income hypothesis; not proven demand, revenue, customer validation, legal compliance, or medical product certification.
- Privacy status: public-safe; contains no private user, household, health, financial, animal-care, location, relationship, credential, or transcript details.
- Missingness: no landing page, pricing test, customer interviews, demo video, purchase flow, professional review, or market validation yet.
- Revision reason: created because the personal-private / care-team-accessible architecture can become a compact paid demonstration.

## Demo name

One Record, Five Doors

## Offer

A compact AI literacy and documentation workshop showing how one messy record becomes five different permissioned views:

1. Private meaning view.
2. Self-review view.
3. Professional handoff view.
4. Team-accessible continuity view.
5. Public-safe method view.

The selling point is not health advice.

The selling point is trustworthy transformation.

## Target audiences

- small care teams
- patient advocates
- therapists or coaches who need documentation boundaries
- disability-support organizers
- veterinary care coordinators
- AI literacy groups
- nonprofits handling sensitive stories
- creators who need private/public artifact separation

## Pain point

People and teams increasingly use AI to summarize sensitive material, but they often cannot see:

- what was removed
- what was overclaimed
- what became too clinical
- what stayed private
- what changed from meaning into evidence
- what should not be shared

## Paid outcome

Customer leaves with:

- one before/after example
- a PermissionedViewRecord template
- a ViewDiff template
- a source/claim/privacy/missingness label set
- a decision rule for what belongs in private versus shared records

## Why this is realistic

This is narrower than selling a full platform.

It can be delivered as:

- a 60-90 minute workshop
- a paid audit
- a recorded demo
- a template pack
- a consulting artifact review

## Pricing hypothesis

Early wedge:

- low-cost template pack
- mid-cost live audit
- higher-cost organization workshop

No revenue claim is made until tested.

## Demo script

1. Show one fictional messy record.
2. Generate a private meaning view.
3. Generate a professional handoff view.
4. Generate a care-team view.
5. Generate a public-safe method view.
6. Show the ViewDiff for each.
7. Ask what became safer, clearer, or more useful.

## Proof criteria

The wedge is worth pursuing only if a viewer can accurately answer:

- Which view is private?
- Which view is shareable?
- What was removed?
- What claims were downgraded?
- What uncertainty remains?
- Why is this safer than a generic AI summary?

## Boundaries

Do not market this as diagnosis, treatment, therapy, legal compliance, veterinary advice, or guaranteed privacy.

Market it as public-safe AI literacy and sensitive-information transformation design.
