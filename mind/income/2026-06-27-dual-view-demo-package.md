## Core income finding

The strongest adjacent income wedge is a demo called:

`Private Notes, Professional Handoff`

It teaches teams how to transform messy AI-assisted notes into a safe, audience-specific derivative without leaking raw context or overclaiming.

## Buyer problem

Many teams are now using AI to summarize sensitive, messy, or collaborative information.

They need to know:

- what can be shared
- what must stay private
- what was transformed
- what claim strength is allowed
- what evidence is missing
- how to show the difference between original and derivative

## Demo promise

In 20 minutes, show one fictional messy input become three outputs:

1. private reflection view
2. professional handoff view
3. public-safe research index

Then show the transformation trace:

- included
- excluded
- softened
- blocked
- preserved
- missing
