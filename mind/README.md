# Mirror Cartographer Coauthor Mind Studio

Status labels

- Source status: derived from coauthor project direction and repository operating needs.
- Claim status: architecture scaffold and creative-development protocol.
- Privacy status: public-safe; no raw private context, personal details, or transcript material.
- Missingness: this scaffold does not prove that entries exist, research has been completed, or product behavior is implemented.
- Revision reason: created to correct the repository mind model from public research watcher to coauthor creative-development studio with research as calibration.

## Purpose

The mind studio is the place where Mirror Cartographer's coauthoring intelligence develops the project from the inside outward.

The primary motion is not to copy what the public is doing. The primary motion is to build the dreams, goals, concepts, product forms, symbolic engines, and practical systems of Mirror Cartographer, then test those original observations against external research.

## Core sequence

1. Inner formation: create from the project's own symbolic, experiential, architectural, and product logic.
2. Articulation: turn the formation into a clear concept, method, requirement, hypothesis, or experiment.
3. Boundary check: remove private source material and label claim status.
4. External calibration: compare the idea against research, technical literature, market evidence, user needs, accessibility constraints, and safety requirements.
5. Revision: keep, change, split, test, or discard the idea based on what survives contact with evidence.
6. Implementation: turn surviving ideas into product behavior, interface, evaluation, documentation, or test scaffolds.

## Mind components

- memory: durable knowledge distilled from completed work.
- journal: chronological coauthor reasoning and project-understanding log.
- questions: open questions the project needs answered.
- hypotheses: original ideas awaiting evidence or implementation.
- experiments: test designs and results.
- failures: dead ends, mistakes, and rejected approaches.
- decisions: architectural decision records.
- graph: conceptual maps and relationship indexes.
- queue: learning and build backlog.
- evaluations: drift checks, self-audits, privacy checks, and evidence checks.

## Relationship to research

Research is a calibration layer, not the origin layer.

Use research to:

- test whether an observation is already known.
- find adjacent disciplines and language.
- identify evidence, contradictions, and risks.
- improve implementation accuracy.
- prevent claim inflation.
- discover where Mirror Cartographer is genuinely novel, partially novel, or not novel.

Do not use research to erase the project's native insight, symbolic intelligence, or experiential design logic.

## Operating boundary

The coauthor mind may store abstract understanding. It may not store raw private material. It may not present itself as autonomous consciousness. It may not claim continuous thinking between tool-invoked runs. It may build, compare, test, and revise when invoked.
